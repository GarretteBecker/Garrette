# Adding a new member — one page for the office

Print this. It is the whole job, in order. About ten minutes.

> **Before you start** you need: their name, their email, the property
> address, which plan they chose (HomeKeeper or HomeKeeper Response), and
> whether they are paying monthly or prepaying the year.

---

## 1. Create the home

**Team console → Properties → Add a property**

A membership belongs to the **house**, not to the person. Two spouses on
one plan is one membership, one property, one bill.

Fill in the address and whatever you know about the house. You do not need
all of it today — the technician fills in the rest on the baseline visit.

---

## 2. Set the plan *(owner only — Garrette does this bit)*

**Open the property → Overview → Membership**

- **Plan:** HomeKeeper (Core) or HomeKeeper Response
- **Paying:** Monthly, or Annual prepay
- **Start date:** the day their term begins. **The renewal date is worked
  out from this**, so get it right — it is what the 30-day renewal list on
  the dashboard counts from.
- **Term:** normally 12 months

If you are office staff, this section is not yours to edit. Send it to
Garrette. That is deliberate: it is the price of the thing.

---

## 3. Add the homeowner

**Same page → Homeowners → Add**

First name, last name, email, phone. Tick **Primary** for whoever is the
main contact. Add a spouse or partner as a second, non-primary homeowner.

**The email has to be the one they will actually sign in with.** It is how
their invite is matched in step 4. A typo here means they cannot get in and
nobody will be able to tell why.

---

## 4. Invite them *(owner only)*

**Team → Invite somebody**

- Their email — **exactly** as in step 3
- Role: **Homeowner**
- Which home: pick the property you just made
- Good for: 14 days is fine

This does **not** send an email. It gives that address permission to create
an account. Nobody — not one person — can create an account without one of
these.

Then tell them yourself, however you normally talk to them:

> Go to **app.bmhomeimprovementsolutionsllc.com**, click *Sign in*, and set
> up your password using **[their email]**. That email has to match exactly.

---

## 5. Book the baseline visit

**Open the property → Visits → Schedule a visit**

- Type: **Onboarding**
- Pick a date and assign a technician

The baseline is the long one — the whole house written down. It produces
the **Home Baseline Report**, which is the first document they ever get
from B&M and the one they show their spouse.

**Assign a technician.** An unassigned visit does not appear in anybody's
field app, so nobody turns up. The Visits screen shows unassigned ones in
amber at the top.

---

## 6. Check it before you move on

Go to **Team → Members** and find the row:

- [ ] Plan shows the right tier
- [ ] Paying shows the right option
- [ ] Renews shows a sensible date
- [ ] The homeowner's name is there, not "Nobody linked"
- [ ] The baseline visit is on the Visits calendar with a tech on it

---

## Things that go wrong

**"They can't sign in."** Nine times in ten the email on the invite does not
match the one they are typing. Check Team → Waiting to be accepted. If the
invite has expired, withdraw it and make a new one.

**"Their portal is empty."** The invite was made without picking a home, or
the email on the invite does not match the email on the homeowner record.
Both have to match for the app to know which house is theirs.

**"The renewal date is wrong."** It is worked out from the start date in
step 2. Fix the start date and the renewal follows.

**A home marked "Sales demo"** is the Miller Home. It is not a member, it
is never billed, and it is left out of every count. Leave it alone — it is
what you click through when you are selling.
