Hard water and other water conditions show up in small, frustrating ways — on glass, fixtures, dishes, appliances, and in the water you drink. Here’s what homeowners notice most.

  • Mineral buildup

  • Appliance scale

Spots & Soap Residue

Minerals show up on the surfaces you clean most often.

  • Hard water spots on shower glass
  • Spots on dishes
  • Soap residue

Taste, Odor & Well Water

City Water vs. Well Water

Your Water Source Matters.

Homes on public water and homes on private wells often face different concerns — and need different solutions.

City Water

Public water is treated by your water utility before it reaches your home. Many homeowners on city water still notice:

  • Hardness
  • Chlorine taste or odor
  • Other taste or odor concerns
  • Scale on fixtures and appliances

Common approaches include water softening, whole-home carbon filtration, or both — chosen based on your water test.

Well Water

Private wells vary widely from home to home. Common well-water concerns include:

  • Hardness
  • Minerals
  • Staining
  • Odors

With a well, evaluation comes first. We test and evaluate the water conditions first, then determine the appropriate next step. Certain water-quality concerns may require certified laboratory testing before an appropriate treatment solution can be recommended.
